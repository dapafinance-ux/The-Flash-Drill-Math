How it works

Display time shrinks 8% after each correct answer and grows 35% after a miss, so it settles where you're right about 78% of the time. That level is your threshold (median display time over your last 10 problems). Lower is better.

Answers submit automatically once you've typed as many characters as the answer has. A miss shows the correct answer for 0.9 s, which counts against the clock.

Answer deadline: the bar under the answer box drains from the moment the problem appears. If you haven't finished typing when it empties, the problem counts as a miss ("too slow") and display time grows. This stops you from holding the image and reading it back slowly. Each deadline keeps its own history and threshold. Lower the deadline by 0.5 s once your average response is under about 70% of it for 3 sessions in a row.

Regroup facts: only the 45 subtraction facts that need a borrow (10 − 1 … 18 − 9) and/or the 45 addition facts that carry (1 + 9 … 9 + 9). The drill remembers how fast you answer each one and shows your slowest ones more often until they're as fast as the rest. The results screen lists your slowest facts.

Esc quits without saving. History is stored in this browser only. Use Export CSV to copy it into your spreadsheet.
